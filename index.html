The Challenge

Why This Problem Must Be Solved Now

Urban neighbourhoods across India face persistent waste management challenges. Swachh Mitra is built to address every one of them.

Open Dumping at Undesignated Spots
Waste accumulates at random locations because designated bins are overflowing or too far from residents.
Lack of Convenient Disposal Points
Communities lack adequate bins in accessible locations, leading to household waste accumulation.
Wet and Dry Waste Mixed Together
Poor segregation at source makes recycling nearly impossible and increases processing costs for municipalities.
Fixed-Schedule Collection Inefficiency
Collection teams follow fixed routes regardless of fill levels, wasting fuel and missing critical overflows.
Overflow Goes Unnoticed
Without real-time monitoring, overflowing bins stay unattended for hours, creating hygiene risks.
Limited Citizen Motivation
Residents have no feedback loop or incentive to maintain cleanliness, leading to low community participation.
Solution

How Swachh Mitra Works

A connected platform linking citizens, smart bins, municipal teams, and communities into one clean loop.

1
Dispose
Citizens deposit segregated wet and dry waste into clearly marked community bins near their homes.
2
Monitor
IoT fill-level sensors continuously track each bin's status and send real-time updates to the dashboard.
3
Predict
Algorithms forecast when each bin will reach capacity, enabling proactive collection scheduling.
4
Collect
Collection teams receive prioritized assignments based on urgency, optimizing routes and response time.
5
Improve
Area scores encourage healthy civic competition, fostering pride and sustained improvement over time.
